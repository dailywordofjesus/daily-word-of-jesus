He Had No Food for Tomorrow — Then He Was Cast to Play Jesus

August 17, 2026
“Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.”
— Proverbs 3:5-6

Millions of people around the world now know Jonathan Roumie as the actor who plays Jesus Christ in the hit series 'The Chosen.' But just months before he was cast in that role, his life looked nothing like success.

Nearly Two Decades of Trying

Roumie had spent almost 20 years chasing an acting career — a decade working as a talent scout and voice actor in New York, followed by eight more years pursuing roles in Los Angeles. Despite all that time and effort, the breakthrough never came.

By the time he reached his lowest point, he was completely out of money, deeply in debt, and did not know where his next meal would come from.

The Moment of Surrender

Rather than continuing to fight for control over his career and circumstances, Roumie made a decision that would define the rest of his life. He got on his knees and prayed, telling God he was placing everything in His hands and would stop trying to force his own way forward.

He has since described that moment as the point where he finally stopped trying to control his own destiny.

Three Months Later

About three months after that prayer of total surrender, Jonathan Roumie was cast as Jesus Christ in 'The Chosen' — a role that would go on to reach hundreds of millions of viewers worldwide and become one of the most successful faith-based series ever made.

Roumie has since reflected that experiencing success earlier in his career might have kept him from developing the depth of faith he has now. He believes the years of hardship were part of God shaping him to depend fully on his faith.

Staying Grounded After Fame

Even after becoming a household name, Roumie has spoken about the danger of letting praise and success go to his head. He has said that staying in prayer and connected to his faith is what allows him to remain surrendered to God, rather than being swept up by attention or compliments.

Now openly living as a devout Catholic, Roumie leads rosaries and novenas, reads Scripture publicly, and speaks candidly about his faith journey whenever he has the opportunity.

The Questions That Remain Unanswered

Roumie has admitted that even now, he doesn't have answers to every 'why' in his life. But he has said that understanding the reason no longer matters to him — what matters is trusting that God knows the plan, even when he himself does not.

What His Story Teaches Us

Jonathan Roumie's journey is a reminder that seasons of emptiness — financial, professional, or emotional — are not evidence that God has forgotten us. Sometimes the very things we are trying hardest to control are the things God is waiting for us to surrender.

If you are in a season where nothing seems to be working no matter how hard you try, this story is proof that surrender is not the end of the road. Sometimes, it's the beginning of the story you were always meant to live.
