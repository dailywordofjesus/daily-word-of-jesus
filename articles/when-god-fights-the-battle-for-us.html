When God Fights the Battle for Us

August 14, 2026
“The Lord will fight for you; you need only to be still.”
— Exodus 14:14

The Beginning: An Unusual Pain

In late 2019, I began experiencing sudden, sharp pain in the area of my skull where a plate had been surgically implanted. At first, it felt strange—pain I was used to typically occurred around the incision site, not the implanted area. But since the discomfort came and went, lasting only a few minutes each time, I chose to ignore it.

Months passed, and a small red bump appeared on my head. As the weeks turned into months, the bump grew larger and the pain intensified.

November 2020: A Screw Works Its Way Out

One day, a tiny, gleaming screw emerged from the bump. I was bewildered—how could a screw come out of my head? Still, I didn't dwell on it. The important thing was that whatever had been causing the pain was finally out.

But days later, the wound swelled with fluid and refused to heal. I went to a local clinic, where the doctor diagnosed an infection, prescribed medication, and asked me to return once the swelling subsided.

A week later, I went back. The clinic doctor referred me to a surgeon at Hermina Hospital because the wound was far deeper than expected. But in the midst of a global pandemic, fear of contracting Covid-19 kept me away from the hospital. I decided to treat it myself at home.

The Skull Bone That Broke by God's Hand

After about a week of home treatment, the wound began to heal. But at the same time, a piece of my skull bone started pushing its way out of my head. I still refused to go to the hospital. If I caught the virus, it could affect my entire family. I held on to one conviction: doctors are merely instruments; it is God who heals.

And then the miracle happened. That incredibly hard piece of skull bone snapped on its own and came out of my head—no surgery, no hospital. The wound closed perfectly. Completely healed.

March 2021: God's Final Surprise

I thought it was all over. But God had one more surprise in store.

The wound reopened. Another hard object emerged—something I assumed was another piece of skull bone. On Saturday, March 6, 2021, the object pushed further out. I pulled it—and realized it wasn't bone at all. It was a long, thin piece of metal. My head felt like it was being sliced as I draw it out. Only half came free; the rest was lodged inside, leaving the metal dangling from my skull. I had no choice but to go to the hospital.

The Road to the Operating Room

That night, I was taken to the emergency room at RSSA Hospital. Since it wasn't classified as an emergency, I was advised to use the outpatient route so my national health insurance (BPJS) would cover it.

On Monday, March 8, I went to Hermina Hospital. The surgeon there couldn't handle the case and referred me to a neurosurgeon at RSSA.

My heart was heavy with worry. RSSA was a designated Covid referral hospital. But the Holy Spirit strengthened me. I believed: The God who sent me to this hospital is the same God who will protect me from every virus and disease.

Tuesday, March 9, 2021

At the RSSA outpatient clinic, something unexpected happened. The doctor immediately scheduled surgery for that very day and admitted me for inpatient care. I was stunned—in my previous experience at RSSA, surgery queues could stretch for weeks.

My pastor prayed for me that same day. Yet God had a different plan: the scheduled surgery fell through and had to be postponed. I didn't complain. I simply prayed, "Your will be done, Lord," and my heart remained at peace.

The Miraculous Friday

The doctor rescheduled the operation for Monday, March 15. But deep in my spirit, I held a conviction: I would be operated on Friday, March 12. Nothing is impossible with God.

On Thursday, I attended prayer and fasting services—sessions I and III. I was renewed with strength. By Thursday evening, there were still no signs the schedule would change. Yet my faith did not waver.

Friday morning, around 9:00 AM, the doctor suddenly announced: "You're having surgery this afternoon. Please begin fasting."

There was even a brief threat of cancellation due to an internal issue in the operating room. But God intervened in a miraculous way. The surgery went ahead and was completed successfully.

Reflection

Through this entire journey, God shaped me to surrender completely to Him—without complaint, without demands. God never deceives us. Through faithfulness within our spiritual community, we are set apart by God, and nothing becomes too heavy to bear, because God fights the battle on our behalf.

A Word of Gratitude

Thank you to my pastors, the congregation, and my fellow young people for every prayer and every word of care. Without that support in prayer, it would have been impossible for my surgery to take place that day. May the Lord repay you with blessings multiplied many times over.

May this testimony be a blessing to all who read it.

God bless you.
