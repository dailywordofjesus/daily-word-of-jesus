Christian News Around the World: 5 Recent Stories Worth Knowing

August 22, 2026
“Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.”
— Matthew 28:19

The global church is always in motion — growing, healing, reconciling, and showing up in places of crisis. Here are five recent stories from around the world worth knowing about, and praying over.

1. A Biblical Film Is Breaking Box Office Records

The animated film 'David', based on the life of the biblical king, has begun screening in cinemas across the UK and Ireland following a successful international run that has already brought in nearly $88 million at the global box office. Its success is a reminder that stories of faith still resonate deeply with audiences far beyond church walls.

2. Two Church Leaders Freed After Being Held Captive in Sudan

Two church leaders who were abducted in Sudan have been released and reunited with their communities. Amid ongoing conflict in the region, their safe return is a welcome answer to the prayers of believers who have been holding them up for weeks.

3. Christian and Muslim Leaders Sign a Peace Accord in Nigeria

More than 50 Christian and Muslim leaders in Nigeria have signed a peace accord aimed at promoting reconciliation between communities that have faced years of tension and violence. It's a picture of what's possible when faith leaders choose the harder, slower work of peace over division.

4. The Global Church Continues to Grow — and Shift

A new report on global Christianity shows there are now more than 2.67 billion Christians worldwide, up from around 2.5 billion just a few years ago. Perhaps more notable than the growth itself is where it's happening: the center of the Christian faith continues shifting away from the Global North and toward the Global South — Africa, Asia, and Latin America — a reminder that the Great Commission is still very much in motion. "Go and make disciples of all nations," Jesus said (Matthew 28:19), and the numbers suggest the Church is still doing exactly that.

5. Operation Blessing Marks 30 Years of Service in the Philippines

Operation Blessing Philippines is celebrating three decades of Christ-centered humanitarian work, having brought hope, healing, and practical help to communities affected by disasters, poverty, and crisis. Thirty years of quiet, faithful service is its own kind of testimony.

A Church Still at Work

Taken together, these stories are a small snapshot of something bigger: a global church that is still growing, still reconciling enemies, still showing up for the suffering, and still finding ways to tell an old story in new ways. Wherever you are today, you're part of that same, much larger story.
