The Lamp on the Stand

No one lights a lamp only to hide it — and yet how often we do exactly that with the small light we've been given: the kind word held back, the gift never offered, the encouragement we decide isn't needed.

"Nor do people light a lamp and put it under a basket... instead they put it on its stand." — Matthew 5:15

A lamp doesn't light a room by being impressive. It lights a room simply by being uncovered. The instruction was never to burn brighter than we're able — only to stop hiding what's already burning.

What is the small, ordinary light you've been keeping under the basket? Today might be the day to set it on the stand.
