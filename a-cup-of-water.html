DAY 003 · AUGUST 03

A Cup of Water

She expected nothing from the stranger at the well — not water, and certainly not to be seen so completely. She had come at noon, the hottest hour, precisely to avoid the other women and their questions.

"Give me a drink." — John 4:7

It was such an ordinary request. But in asking her for water, he gave her something rarer than water: the experience of being spoken to honestly, without judgment, by someone who already knew everything about her and stayed anyway.

Sometimes the smallest request — a favor, a conversation, a shared cup — is the doorway into being fully known. Don't be afraid of the ordinary moment; it may be carrying more than it appears to.
