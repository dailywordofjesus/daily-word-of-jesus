The One Who Was Sought

Out of ninety-nine safe and counted, it was the single missing one that moved the shepherd to walk into the dark. Not because the ninety-nine mattered less, but because being counted was never the point of the flock — belonging was.

"What man of you, having a hundred sheep, if he loses one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine... and go after the one that is lost?" — Luke 15:4

We tend to measure our worth by the group we're safely inside of. But this story turns that logic inside out: the shepherd's attention doesn't average itself across the flock. It goes fully, entirely, to whoever has wandered.

If tonight you feel like the one who wandered off, this is the story that says you were never left to find your own way back.
