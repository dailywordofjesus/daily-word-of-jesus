DAY 001 · AUGUST 05

Be Still and Know

There are mornings when the noise of the world arrives before we're ready for it — a notification, a worry, a list of things undone — and it is in that very noise that a quieter voice asks us to pause.

"Be still, and know that I am God." — Psalm 46:10

A fisherman named Peter once spent a whole night with empty nets. He knew the lake, he knew his craft, and still the water gave him nothing. It would have been easy to call the night a failure and go home. Instead, he stayed one moment longer, and in that moment he was asked to try again, in a way that made no sense to him.

Stillness is not the absence of effort. It is the space we make before the next right step — the pause between the empty net and the full one. It is choosing, for one breath, to stop narrating the outcome and simply notice who is standing in the boat with us.

Today, before you reach for the next task, try one still moment. Not to escape the day, but to remember that you are not carrying it alone.
